## What is 360 management software?

**360 management software** is an all-in-one operating system for selling plotted real estate. Instead of a CRM for leads, a spreadsheet for inventory, PDFs for brokers, and another tool for payments, one platform owns the full sales cycle around a live digital layout.

Layouts360 is **360 management software** purpose-built for Indian layout and township developers. Every plot on the map carries status, buyer interest, booking state, and payment history—so sales, finance, and partners work from the same truth.

**In one sentence:** 360 management software replaces Excel + WhatsApp + generic CRM with live maps, inventory, CRM, bookings, and partner portals in one Layouts360 account.

## Who is this for?

### Layout developers and land promoters

You need the site plan itself to drive sales—not a contact list that ignores which plots are blocked. 360 management software digitizes the layout and keeps every enquiry tied to real units.

### Builders with plotted or mixed inventory

Plots, villas, and commercial corners should share one CRM and booking engine. Fragmented tools create double bookings and broker disputes.

### Sales desks and field teams

Site offices need mobile inventory and pipeline on the same screen. Managers need dashboards that match what agents see on the land.

### Channel partners and broker networks

Brokers need a live portal. 360 management includes partner access so PDFs stop circulating after every status change.

## Common questions about 360 management software

### Is 360 management just branding for a CRM?

No. CRM is one module. 360 management also owns the digital layout, inventory states, booking rules, partner access, and payment milestones on the same plot IDs.

### Can we keep Excel for inventory and only buy CRM?

You can, but you recreate the problem 360 software solves. Parallel inventory sheets are how double bookings and broker disputes start.

### Does 360 management replace accounting software?

It tracks booking amounts, installments, and GST for plot sales. Deep general ledger work may still live in Tally or your CA’s tools—Layouts360 keeps sales-side collections tied to inventory.

### How is this different from township management software?

[Plot management software](/real-estate-plot-management-software) covers multi-phase layouts and townships. 360 management is the broader suite story—maps through partners—for any layout size, including single-phase projects.

### What should we bring to a demo?

Your layout file (CAD or PDF), a sample plot list with pricing, and one painful launch or broker story. Ask vendors to block a plot and show the partner view update live.

## Problems teams face without 360 management

### Tool sprawl

Leads in one app, inventory in Excel, brokers on WhatsApp, payments in a ledger—nobody trusts the “current” file. Coordinators become human APIs between systems that never quite agree.

### Launch-day chaos

Without timed blocks and live maps, high-demand weekends produce double allotments and angry partners. A war room of printed sheets cannot scale past a few dozen concurrent buyers.

### Inventory lies

CRM stages claim a deal is open while the plot sold an hour ago on another desk’s sheet. Managers coach on fiction, and buyers lose trust when status flips after a site visit.

### Partner distrust

Brokers lose faith when yesterday’s PDF still shows available plots that closed overnight. Top partners quietly push competitors who pay faster and show cleaner inventory.

### Hidden cost of “cheap” stacks

A low-cost CRM plus coordinator overtime plus dispute refunds often costs more than one affordable 360 platform. Affordability should include the cost of wrong bookings, not only the monthly subscription.

### Handoff gaps between sales and finance

When booking amounts live in chat and installment schedules live in a separate ledger, outstanding balances drift. Buyers get conflicting statements; leadership cannot answer “how much is collected on Phase 2?” without a weekend merge.

## Rollout playbook for 360 management

**Week 1:** Digitize one active layout, import the plot master, and align status labels with how your desk already talks (available, blocked, booked, sold).

**Week 2:** Train internal sales on mobile map + CRM. Turn off the shadow spreadsheet for that project—or freeze it as read-only archive.

**Week 3:** Onboard a pilot set of channel partners to the live portal. Compare booking request quality against WhatsApp PDF week.

**Month 1:** Review double-booking incidents, partner questions, and time-to-block. Expand to the next phase or project only after the first layout is the single source of truth.

**Red flags:** A parallel Excel “just for launch day”; partners still receiving weekly PDF dumps; CRM stages that do not reference plot IDs; finance collecting payments without booking references in the system.
