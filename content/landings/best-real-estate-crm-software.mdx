## Common questions about the best real estate CRM in India

### What makes a CRM "best" for plot developers?

Plot hierarchy, live maps, mobile field usage, partner visibility, and booking conversion—not email automation alone.

### Should international CRMs qualify?

Only if they demonstrate plot-level inventory and Indian installment workflows in your first demo with your layout file present.

### How do you compare vendors?

Score live block-to-booking in session, partner view update, and finance balance per plot—fail vendors that only show contact screens.

### Is price per seat important?

Unlimited users on tiers suit growing broker networks better than per-seat global pricing that punishes scale.

### What is a red flag?

Demos without inventory locks or requiring months-long implementation before the next launch you already scheduled.



### Is mobile mandatory?

Yes for Indian plot sales—site offices and brokers sell on phones. Desktop-only CRMs become coordinator tools, not field systems.

### What question ends a vendor demo?

"Show me partner view updating when I block Plot 22 on this layout." Failure to demonstrate is a fail.

## Rollout playbook for CRM selection

**Week 1:** Scorecard: plot hierarchy, live map, block-to-book in demo, partner view, installment on plot. Disqualify vendors missing any in first session.

**Week 2:** Pilot one live project with five agents and two partners—not a sandbox with fake names. Measure adoption Saturday at site office.

**Month 1:** Compare booked plots and dispute rate versus prior Excel period. Selection success is usage on launch day, not feature checklist alone.

**Red flags:** Buying per-seat global CRM to bend with custom fields; implementation longer than next scheduled launch; brokers never receive portal login. Best CRM for India plot sales is used on the layout, not in HQ only.



### Handoff to leadership

Vendor selection committee includes site manager and one senior broker—not only IT and marketing. Pilot success criteria written before contract: Saturday adoption, block-to-book on map, partner view live. Fail fast on vendors that require inventory in Excel sidecar.

### Training rhythm
