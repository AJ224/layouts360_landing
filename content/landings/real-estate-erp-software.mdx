## What is real estate ERP software?

**Real estate ERP software** for developers typically means unifying sales operations: maps, inventory, CRM, bookings, installments, and partner workflows—not tenant billing or statutory GL alone.

Layouts360 defines **sales ERP** as: inventory, bookings, payments, partners, documents—not replacement for Tally.

**In one sentence:** real estate ERP software helps Indian developers sell plots and units with live inventory, digital layouts, and connected bookings—not disconnected spreadsheets.

## Who is this for?

### Layout developers and land promoters

You sell phased layouts where plot numbers, facing, and live availability matter more than generic contact records. real estate ERP software maps every enquiry to real units on a digital layout.

### Builders and villa project owners

Builders running plotted phases or mixed inventory need one system for sales teams, finance visibility, and broker coordination without rebuilding Excel every weekend.

### Real estate sales teams

Field agents need mobile access at the site office. Managers need pipeline and inventory truth in the same dashboard. sales ERP software should work where deals actually close—on phones and tablets.

### Channel partners and broker networks

If brokers sell your inventory, they need a live portal—not PDFs in WhatsApp. Partner workflows belong in the same platform as your internal sales desk.

Across India, layout developers in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities scale broker networks faster than back-office headcount. real estate ERP software should scale partner access without scaling spreadsheet chaos.

**Week 1:** Document must-have sales workflows: map, block, partner request, installment. Compare ERP demo to that list before finance module depth.

**Week 2:** Pilot sales operations only—agents, partners, bookings. Defer GL migration if statutory books already stable in existing tools.

**Month 1:** Export booking and collection data to accounting on agreed cadence. Measure agent adoption before expanding module scope.

**Red flags:** ERP purchased for leadership reporting while agents use WhatsApp; twelve-month implementation misses next launch; inventory module treated as optional add-on. Sales-side ERP wins when launch weekend runs on it.
