## What is real estate CRM software?

**Real estate CRM software** organizes inquiries, follow-ups, site visits, and pipeline stages from first call to booking. For plot and layout developers, CRM must understand plot numbers, phases, and live availability—not only contact names.

A contact list without inventory context is not a builder CRM. It is a phone book that creates false promises.

**In one sentence:** real estate CRM software for Indian developers ties every lead to live plot inventory, digital layouts, and booking—so pipeline matches what you can still sell.

## CRM for plotted development (not rental, not agency-only)

**CRM for plotted development** is built for layout and land sales:

- Every enquiry maps to a plot number, facing, and live status
- Site visits log *which plots were shown*
- Blocks and tokens are pipeline events—not vague “proposal sent” stages
- Brokers share lead ownership rules on the same inventory

Generic realtor CRMs track contacts and deal stages for brokerage. Plotted-development CRM must also know which plots are blocked, which partners own which leads, and what remains in the next phase.
