### Can realtors work only on mobile?

Yes for day-to-day site work. Managers may use larger screens for roll-ups; agents should not need a laptop on the plot.

CRM ownership and timestamps reduce fights. Inventory holds prevent two verbal promises on one plot.

### Do external brokers count as realtors here?

In practice they sell similarly on site. Give them [channel partner](/real-estate-channel-partner-management-software) access so they see live inventory without full internal permissions.

### Will agents reject a new CRM?

Adoption sticks when the app is faster than WhatsApp for “is it open?” If the tool is slower, they will revert—demo with real plots.

### Is offline required?

Layouts360 is cloud-first so everyone sees current status. Site offices need data connectivity; that is the trade for live inventory truth.

## Rollout playbook for agent CRM adoption

**Week 1:** Pick two strong agents as champions. Digitize their active project and practice site-visit logging on real plots.

**Week 2:** Migrate open leads with plot interest fields. Ban new leads in personal notebooks for that project.

**Week 3:** Add remaining realtors. Run a short daily map huddle (15 minutes) instead of a long weekly pipeline meeting.

**Month 1:** Review collision incidents and time-to-hold. Expand partner portal only after internal agents trust the map.

**Red flags:** Agents still keep a parallel Excel; managers ask for WhatsApp screenshots of availability; new temporary realtors excluded “to save seats”; CRM stages with no plot ID required.

Affordable CRM for realtors only works when every agent opens the live map before promising a plot. If your desk still answers “let me check Excel,” the CRM is not yet the system of record—fix that habit before scaling marketing spend on the next phase.
