## What Is Real Estate Sales Analytics?

**Real estate sales analytics** means tracking how buyers interact with your inventory—which plots they zoom into, how long they stay on a layout map, which price band they filter—and turning that behavior into actionable sales intelligence.

For **layout developers** and **plot selling companies**, this is not vanity metrics. It tells you which facing sells fastest, which marketing channel brings serious buyers, and which agent should call whom first.
