## Evaluation checklist before you buy

- Upload your actual layout PDF or CAD and confirm plot-level clicks work
- Run a timed block with two agents on the same plot
- Invite one broker to the partner portal—not a PDF attachment test
- Export a leadership dashboard without manual sheet merge
- Confirm INR pricing, support hours, and who trains site staff
