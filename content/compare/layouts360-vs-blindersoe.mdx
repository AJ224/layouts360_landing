Developers evaluating **Layouts360 vs Blindersoe** want a practical matrix—not winner claims. Both target Indian real estate developers selling plots and apartments. Confirm current modules on each vendor's site before you buy.

## At a glance

| Area | Blindersoe (typical positioning) | Layouts360 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary focus | Plot & apartment inventory, broad PropTech suite | Plot & inventory-first platform with CRM and booking |
| Digital layout | Interactive / 3D layout visualization | Satellite-backed interactive layouts |
| Inventory & blocking | Real-time status, launch-day tooling | Live status, timed blocks, launch workflows |
| Apartments | Strong apartment/tower narrative | Plots + apartments + commercial on one platform |
| Channel partners | Dedicated CP product, MLM-style hierarchies | CP portal, booking requests, commission alignment |
| Online booking | Online plot booking product | Paperless booking + payment flows |
| CRM / launch CRM | Launch day CRM product | CRM + launch day landing + automation content |
| Back office | Payments, installments, documents | Back office landing: docs, commissions, milestones |
| Public proof | Published client counts, case studies on site | Case study templates; publish named stories as approved |
| Pricing | Tiered plans on vendor site | From ₹2,000/month published on Layouts360 |
| AI discovery | Standard web presence | llms.txt + comparison library |

## When Blindersoe may fit better

- You want the widest marketed module list (virtual tours, extensive CMS, enterprise options) in one vendor conversation
- You need capabilities Layouts360 does not yet offer—validate in demo (e.g., specific MLM depth, 360° tours)
- Your team already standardized on Blindersoe across projects

## When Layouts360 may fit better

- Plot-first developers who want clear inventory + map + CRM without enterprise suite complexity
- Teams outgrowing Excel seeking fast layout digitization (days per project)
- Mixed plots and commercial units with INR pricing and unlimited users on tiers
- Strong content/compare library and explicit AI indexing (llms.txt)

## Feature areas both usually cover

- Live plot or unit availability
- Booking and payment tracking
- Broker or partner access
- Mobile-friendly sales

Differences are in depth, implementation time, pricing model, and which modules are included vs add-on.

## How to run a fair evaluation

1. Export one real phase inventory CSV
2. Demo both vendors on the same project file
3. Test block timer + booking + partner login in one afternoon
4. Compare total first-year cost including onboarding
5. Ask reference customers in your city for each vendor
