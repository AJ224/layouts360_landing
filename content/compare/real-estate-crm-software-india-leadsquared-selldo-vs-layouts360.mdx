Teams comparing **real estate CRM software in India** often shortlist marketing-heavy suites and apartment-first PropTech. Plot and layout developers need a different lens: **live map, plot ID, timed blocks, broker portal**.

## What large PropTech CRMs emphasize

[LeadSquared Real Estate CRM](https://www.leadsquared.com/real-estate-crm/) focuses on lead capture, nurture, inventory mapping, mobile field force, and campaign analytics—strong for inquiry volume and buyer engagement.

[Sell.do Real Estate CRM](https://www.sell.do/real-estate-crm) bundles sales automation, towers/units inventory, WhatsApp, channel partners, and post-sales ledgers—strong for structured apartment and broker operations.

Listicles such as PropFlo-style **top CRM in India** posts often rank similar platforms. Useful for **flat and broker-led** motions; verify **open plot layouts** and **launch blocking** in your demo.

## When a marketing CRM is enough

- Mostly apartment towers with floor PDFs  
- Low broker count, desk-controlled inventory  
- CRM goal = nurture and call center, not map selling  

## When layout-first wins

- Phased plotted layouts and townships  
- Partner networks selling live inventory  
- Launch days with concurrent buyers  
- Mixed plots + villas + commercial in one brand
