## The 8 operating leaks (with the ₹ math)

### The coordinator who became a human database

**8–20 hrs/week chasing “is Plot X free?” — ₹2–4L/yr of payroll doing what a lock should do**

Someone on your team updates Version 6 of Excel, forwards PDFs, and answers the same question 20–50 times on a busy Saturday. It looks like “ops support.” It is inventory infrastructure paid as salary.

### Double booking is a P&L event, not an oops

**One disputed allotment on a ₹40L plot can cost more than a year of software**

Refunds, goodwill, broker fallout, two weeks of founder attention. Teams at ~120 plots often see **1–2 incidents a quarter** once brokers scale. That is not bad luck. That is architecture.

### Verbal holds have no timer — so launches invent chaos

**200+ brokers + paper chart = weekend archaeology on Monday**

Whoever shouted first “owns” the plot until the sheet is rebuilt at midnight. You cannot market scarcity you cannot prove.

### WhatsApp PDFs age in minutes

**Hourly blasts to 40+ partners still produce “my PDF was green” fights**

The cost is not data. The cost is manager hours + partner churn. Fair access beats another incentive slab.

### Generic CRM without plot IDs creates false pipeline

**Leads look healthy while inventory lies in Excel**

Managers coach “did you call?” instead of “which plots did you show?” Marketing celebrates volume. Sales refunds trust.

### Leadership flies blind on Phase 2

**Sold % that takes a meeting is not a metric — it is a delay**

Release timing becomes politics. Cash conversion of land you already hold slips by weeks.

### Seat-tax CRMs punish growth

**15 agents + 25 brokers on per-seat tools = surprise bills for a problem they still don’t solve**

If availability is outside the CRM, you bought follow-ups, not a sales system.

### Shadow Excel survives “go-live”

**If the sheet is still called “real,” the project failed — software or not**

Adoption is the product. Locks only work when WhatsApp celebrates bookings *after* status changes in the system.
