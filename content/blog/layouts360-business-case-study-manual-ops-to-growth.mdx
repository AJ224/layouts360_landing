## The business before: real-world manual & staff issues

### Issue 1 — You are paying for a “human database”

In a typical **120-plot** layout, one junior or senior staffer becomes the unofficial inventory server:

- Receives “block Plot 67” WhatsApp messages  
- Updates **Version 6** of the master sheet  
- Forwards a new PDF to **15–40** brokers  
- Answers the same availability question **20–50 times a day** on busy weekends  

That role feels cheap until you cost it honestly:

- **₹25,000–₹45,000 / month** fully loaded for a coordinator (illustrative mid-market India)  
- **~50%+** of their week spent on status chase, not closing or collections  
- Error rate rises exactly when revenue spikes (launches)

**Growth blocker:** Your best people become message routers. Hiring more coordinators does not fix the model—it multiplies versions.

### Issue 2 — Double booking is not a “ops oops”; it is a P&L event

A single double allotment on a **₹35–₹60 lakh** plot can mean:

- Refund / legal / goodwill cost: often **₹50,000–₹5+ lakh** in soft + hard cost  
- Broker relationship damage that kills **5–15** future referrals  
- **1–2 weeks** of management attention diverted from Phase 2 sales  

Teams running Excel + verbal holds commonly report **~2 incidents per quarter** once agent + broker count grows. That is not bad luck. It is architecture.

### Issue 3 — Launch days break your process on purpose

Paper launch rooms with **200+ brokers** create predictable failure:

- Verbal holds with no timer  
- Two desks writing on one chart  
- Partners selling from a PDF printed at **9:00 a.m.** while desk status moved at **9:12 a.m.**  
- Night-long reconciliation to discover what “sold”

**Business cost:** You cannot market scarcity you cannot prove. You also cannot scale launch intensity without scaling chaos.

### Issue 4 — Broker networks stall at the WhatsApp ceiling

Around **40+ partners**, hourly PDF blasts produce:

- Constant “but my PDF showed green” disputes  
- Uneven access (favorites get faster updates)  
- Channel conflict that looks like commission drama but is really **inventory lag**

Partners do not need more incentives first. They need **identical live stock**.

### Issue 5 — CRM without plots creates false pipeline

Staff log calls in Zoho/Excel CRM while availability lives elsewhere. Pipeline looks healthy; inventory is not. Managers coach activity, not **plots shown**. Marketing celebrates lead volume while sales refunds trust.

See [when generic CRM fails plot developers](/blog/when-generic-crm-fails-plot-developers).

### Issue 6 — Leadership flies blind on growth decisions

Questions that should take seconds take meetings:

- Sold % by phase right now?  
- How many plots blocked vs booked vs collectible?  
- Which brokers convert holds?  
- Can we release Phase 2 this month?

Without a live system, Phase release timing is politics + gut feel—not inventory math.

## Staff change management (what actually makes numbers stick)

Software fails when Excel remains the “real” file. Enforce:

1. **Week 1:** Digitize Phase 1; train block-and-book on tablets  
2. **Week 2:** Partner portal live; mock launch (block → expire → book)  
3. **Month 1:** Leadership reviews sold % only from Layouts360  
4. **Hard rule:** WhatsApp may celebrate a booking; it may not define availability  

Red flags: shadow Excel, PDF forwards, holds that never expire.
