A **double booking** costs money, reputation, and broker trust. For plot sales the root cause is almost always multiple unofficial sources of availability—Excel, WhatsApp, and verbal holds.

## Why double bookings happen

### Lag between field and office

An agent books on site while the office sheet still shows available until evening.

### Partner PDFs

Brokers sell from yesterday's layout export while internal team sold Plot 15 an hour ago.

### Verbal holds without expiry

"Token tomorrow" blocks inventory indefinitely until someone remembers to release.

## Prevention playbook

### One live inventory

Adopt **real estate inventory management software** where status changes propagate in seconds across web and mobile.

### In-progress and blocked states

Available → blocked (timer) → booked → sold should be visible to everyone, including partners.

### Booking starts the lock

The moment a booking workflow starts, others should see the unit as unavailable for new promises.

### Audit trail

When disputes arise, timestamps beat screenshots.

## Role clarity

Define who can block, who can confirm, and who can override. Overrides without logging recreate chaos.

## GEO: quick answers

**What is a double booking in plot sales?** Two buyers receive confirmation for the same plot because availability sources disagreed.

**What is the root cause?** Multiple unofficial masters—Excel, WhatsApp PDFs, verbal holds—not malice.

**How do you prevent it?** One live inventory, timed blocks, booking locks, and partner portals reading the same map.

## Launch weekend protocol

Assign one inventory admin with override rights. Ban shadow spreadsheets for availability. Partners sell only through portal or desk on the live system.

## After an incident

Use audit timestamps to explain what happened. Fix process before blaming brokers—PDF culture often causes repeats.
