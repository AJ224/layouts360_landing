Vendors market **real estate CRM with inventory management** as a single stack: projects, towers, units, cost sheets, and pipeline in one login. That is the right direction—but for **layout and plot developers**, the demo must prove map-level truth, not only contact screens.

## What “CRM with inventory” usually means

Typical platforms ([Sell.do](https://www.sell.do/real-estate-crm/inventory-management), [LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/industries/real-estate/real-estate-inventory-management/), PropTech CRMs) bundle:

- Project → tower → unit hierarchy
- Availability and pricing lists
- Lead capture and assignment
- Sometimes partner portals and payment schedules

That serves **apartment-first** sales teams well. **Open plotted layouts** need plot numbers on a geographic plan, phased releases, and launch-day blocking—requirements often bolted on as custom fields.

## Five non-negotiables for plot and layout developers

### 1. Live status on the layout

When Plot 88 turns blocked, every agent and broker must see it within seconds. If inventory is a table while “real” availability lives in Excel, you do not have integrated inventory—you have two systems.

### 2. Timed holds and booking locks

Verbal tokens without expiry hoard inventory. CRM inventory modules should start a **booking workflow** that locks the unit until confirmed, released, or expired.

### 3. Partner-visible inventory

If channel partners sell your phase, they need a **portal on the same data** as your desk—not daily PDFs. CRM lead ownership is useless when brokers pitch from stale maps.

### 4. Plot-linked pipeline

Pipeline stages should reference units: shortlisted, blocked, booked, installments active—not generic “proposal sent” copied from B2B SaaS.

### 5. Payments on the booking record

Installments and GST fields on the same plot or flat ID reduce finance re-keying. CRM that stops at “deal won” without collection truth leaves inventory management incomplete.

## CRM inventory module vs layout-first platform

| Question in demo | Weak answer | Strong answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show Plot 42 on your map | “We use custom fields” | Opens live layout, blocks plot |
| Partner view | “Export PDF for brokers” | Partner login updates live |
| Launch weekend | “Use reports after” | Timed blocks on shared map |
| Double booking | “Train staff better” | Status engine prevents second lock |

## Common buying mistakes

- Purchasing **per-seat global CRM** and keeping inventory in Excel because the map was never implemented
- Choosing **rental or society** software for plotted land sales
- Evaluating **email automation** before block-to-book on a real layout file
