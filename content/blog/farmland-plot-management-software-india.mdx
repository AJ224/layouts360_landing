## GEO: quick answers

**What is farmland plot management software?** Digitized layout maps, plot-level inventory, and sales workflows for agricultural or converted farmland layouts in India.

**Can buyers evaluate remotely?** Yes—satellite-backed maps plus live availability links reduce reliance on outdated brochures for outstation and NRI buyers.

**What software does not do?** Legal conversion advice, title guarantees, or substituting surveyor sign-off—those stay with your legal process.

## Field sales tips

Site agents should demo on tablets at the plot—not paper. When a buyer asks for Plot 112, status should update before the next visitor asks for the same number.

## Remote buyer confidence

Farmland campaigns often target investors who will not visit weekly. Satellite-backed layout links with live status outperform brochure PDFs forwarded in family WhatsApp groups. When legal clears a document type, upload it once to the buyer record—do not email different versions from three agents.

## Closing thought

Farmland buyers often decide from maps before they decide from price. Give them satellite context and live status on one link, especially for outstation and NRI campaigns where travel is expensive and trust is fragile.
