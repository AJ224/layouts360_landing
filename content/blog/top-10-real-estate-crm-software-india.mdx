Lists of **top 10 CRM for real estate** often rank global or apartment-first tools. If you sell **plotted layouts** in India, use this as a **scorecard**—not a vendor pay-to-play list.

## How we structured this “top 10”

Ten **capabilities** matter more than ten brand logos. Score each CRM you demo 0–2 per row (0 = missing, 1 = partial, 2 = proven on your layout).

| # | Capability | Why it matters for plot developers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Plot/unit on live map | Follow-ups reference real availability |
| 2 | Timed block → booking | Launch and site-office discipline |
| 3 | Mobile site office | Deals close on phones at cabin |
| 4 | Partner portal | Brokers off PDF inventory |
| 5 | Lead source + CP rules | Dispute prevention |
| 6 | Multi-project portfolio | No CSV per layout |
| 7 | Booking + installments | Finance on same record |
| 8 | INR pricing model | Predictable cost as team grows |
| 9 | Implementation speed | Live before next launch |
| 10 | India plot sales proof | Demos with *your* map file |

**18+ points** across two sessions with your data → shortlist. **Under 12** → keep looking.

## Categories you will see in India

**PropTech CRMs** ([Sell.do](https://www.sell.do/real-estate-crm), PropFlo-style platforms) — strong on towers, marketing automation, CP workflows. Verify open **plot** layouts and launch blocking.

**Horizontal CRM + customization** (Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce) — flexible contacts; inventory usually external Excel.

**Marketing-heavy suites** ([LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/real-estate-crm/)) — capture, nurture, inventory module; confirm map-native plot sales in demo.

**Purpose-built layout CRM** — **Layouts360**: plot & inventory + CRM + booking for Indian layout developers.

## Red flags when reviewing any “top CRM”

- Demo uses fake project names, not your phase  
- Inventory shown as table only—no layout map  
- “Partner portal” = email PDF export  
- Per-seat pricing that punishes 30 agents  
- No block expiry on holds
