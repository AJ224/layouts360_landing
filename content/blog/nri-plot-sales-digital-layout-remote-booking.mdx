**NRI plot sales** depend on trust before the site visit. Buyers abroad cannot walk the land every weekend. They need a **digital layout** that shows real boundaries, live availability, and a clear path to token or booking—without stale PDFs in family WhatsApp groups.

## Why NRIs abandon offline-only sales

### PDF maps age overnight

A brochure sent on Monday may show Plot 22 available when your office sold it on Sunday. NRIs lose confidence fast.

### Time zones and slow callbacks

When availability checks take 24 hours, buyers move to the next developer who answers with data.

### Payment confusion

Disconnected UPI links without plot linkage create "I paid but the plot is still green" disputes.

## What to offer NRIs digitally

### Satellite-backed interactive layout

Show where the plot sits on real land—not only a CAD sketch. Zoom, pan, and tap plot cards for area and facing.

### Live availability on the same link

Marketing, sales, and the buyer should read one inventory state.

### Secure project link

Share one branded URL on email and WhatsApp. Updates propagate when status changes.

### Remote booking steps

Guided token or booking with receipt and CRM record—see our [online plot booking guide](/blog/online-plot-booking-security-payment-flow).

## Remote booking workflow

1. NRI receives project link from agent or campaign
2. Buyer shortlists plots on mobile
3. Agent confirms on a quick video call if needed
4. Buyer pays booking amount through integrated flow
5. Plot moves to booked; everyone sees the update
