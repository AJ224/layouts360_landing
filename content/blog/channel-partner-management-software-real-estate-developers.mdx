**Channel partner management software** helps real estate developers scale broker networks without losing control of inventory, leads, or commissions. When CP count passes twenty, WhatsApp PDFs and verbal "my buyer" claims stop working.

## What channel partner management includes

### Partner onboarding and access

Individual logins per broker or agency—see only assigned projects.

### Live inventory on digital layout

Partners filter available, blocked, and sold plots in real time.

### Lead and booking requests

Enquiries and booking requests originate from the layout, not screenshots.

### Commission alignment

Payout logic tied to confirmed bookings, not arguments.

## Who needs this first

Developers with active broker networks, multi-city CPs, or launch weekends where partner volume spikes.

## What it is not

Not a replacement for legal CP agreements. Not rental brokerage MLS software.

## Metrics that prove CP software is working

Track partner logins per week, booking requests per partner, and dispute count per launch. If logins are low, training failed—not software. If disputes rise while logins are high, tighten lead tagging rules.

## Contract + software together

Software cannot fix unsigned CP agreements. Combine portal access with written commission slabs and lead ownership windows. Partners accept transparency when numbers match bookings.

## Scaling from 20 to 200 brokers

At scale, assign regional coordinators in software roles. They approve requests and coach sub-brokers. Without role hierarchy, your HQ team becomes the bottleneck.
