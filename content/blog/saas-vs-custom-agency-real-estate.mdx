It starts with a seductive pitch.

A web development agency sits across from you and says, *"We will build a custom solution just for you. Exact to your specifications. You will own the code."*

It sounds perfect. You feel special. You envision a platform that fits your business like a bespoke suit.

**Six months later, the reality hits.** The project is delayed. The budget has doubled. And when you ask for a small change to the booking form, they send you an invoice for ₹15,000.

This is the **Agency Trap**. And for 99% of real estate developers, it is a financial black hole compared to a specialized SaaS platform like Layouts360. Here is the brutal truth about why "Custom" is often a mistake.

## 1. The "Feature Tax" vs. The Innovation Stream

**Agency Model:**
In a custom build, every feature has a price tag.
*   Want a CRM integration? **₹25,000.**
*   Need a new filter for plot sizes? **₹10,000.**
*   Google changed their Maps API? **You pay for the fix.**

You are discouraged from innovating because every idea costs money.

**SaaS Model (Layouts360):**
We have hundreds of clients. When *one* client asks for a brilliant new feature, we build it, and **everyone gets it for free**.
*   We optimized the mobile view last week. **You got it instantly.**
*   We added WhatsApp integration. **No extra charge.**

With SaaS, your platform gets *better* over time without you spending a rupee. With custom code, your platform *decays* over time unless you keep paying.

## 2. Speed: Missing the Season

Real estate is seasonal. If you have a launch in October, you need your system ready in September.

*   **Agency Timeline:** 3-6 months. Discovery, Design, Dev, Testing, Bug Fixing. Agencies are juggling 10 other clients; you are just one of them. If they miss your launch date, you lose sales.
*   **SaaS Timeline:** 3-5 days. The software is already built. We just upload your map, configure your plots, and you are live.

**Speed is revenue.** The months you spend waiting for an agency are months your competitors are closing deals.

## 3. The "Works on My Machine" Reliability

When an agency builds software for *one* client, it is tested by... one client. You are the guinea pig. You will find the bugs during a live customer presentation.

Layouts360 is battle-tested by thousands of users every day.
*   When a button breaks, we know instantly.
*   We have handled high-traffic launches.
*   Our servers are optimized for load.

You aren't paying us to *experiment*; you are paying us for a **proven machine**.

## 4. The Hidden Maintenance Nightmare

Code rots. Servers need security patches. APIs get deprecated.

If you own the code, **you represent the IT department.**
*   Who renews the SSL certificate?
*   Who fixes the database when it crashes on a Sunday?
*   Who secures user data against the latest hack?

With an agency, these are "Change Requests" or costly AMCs (Annual Maintenance Contracts). With Layouts360, it's just **included**. We stay awake faster so you can sleep.

## 5. Cost: The Tip of the Iceberg

**Custom Build:**
*   **Upfront:** ₹2 Lakhs - ₹5 Lakhs
*   **Server/Hosting:** ₹10k/year
*   **AMC/Support:** ₹50k/year
*   **Total Year 1:** **~₹3-5 Lakhs** (and high risk)

**Layouts360:**
*   **Upfront:** Small setup fee.
*   **Monthly:** Low subscription (e.g., ₹2k - ₹4k).
*   **Total Year 1:** **Fraction of the cost.**

Why pay to build the factory when you just want to sell the product?

## Conclusion

The psychological trick of "Custom Development" is that it feeds the ego—it feels good to "own" the software.

But in business, **ownership is a liability. Results are the asset.**

Smart developers realize they aren't software companies. They don't need to own the code; they need to close the deal. Layouts360 gives you a Ferrari for the price of an Uber ride. Don't buy the mechanic's shop.
