This buyer’s checklist is written for Indian layout developers, land promoters, and builders selling plotted phases—not for rental managers or apartment-only brokerages.

## Step 1: Confirm you actually need plot management (not just CRM)

You need dedicated **plot management software** if two or more of these are true:

- Multiple agents or brokers sell the same layout at once  
- You have ever double-booked or “almost” double-booked a plot  
- Availability lives in Excel, WhatsApp, or a PDF map  
- Buyers ask to see plot position on land, not only a number in a sheet  
- Leadership cannot answer “how many left?” without calling three people  

If your pain is only email follow-ups and inventory is already perfect, a lighter CRM might do. Most layout teams do **not** have perfect inventory.

## Step 2: Lock your must-have outcomes

Write outcomes before demos. Example outcomes that matter:

1. One live map every role trusts  
2. Zero verbal holds without timers  
3. Brokers sell from the same inventory as the desk  
4. Leads always reference a plot ID  
5. Go-live in days for one phase—not months  

Vendors that cannot map features to these outcomes are selling modules, not operations.

## Step 3: Score features that change launch-day reality

### Digital layout quality

- CAD/PDF import into an interactive map  
- Satellite context buyers understand  
- Plot cards: number, area, facing, rate, status  

Poor maps = agents return to PDFs within a week.

### Inventory status engine

- Available / blocked / booked / sold (or equivalent)  
- Instant sync on phone and desktop  
- Clear owner of who can change status  

Without this, you bought a pretty picture.

### Timed holds

- Auto-expiry  
- Visible to partners  
- Extension rules your managers control  

Verbal “keep it for him till evening” is how disputes start.

### Plot-linked CRM

- Mandatory plot interest on new leads  
- Site visit logging with plots shown  
- Pipeline stages that match block → token → sold  

See [real estate CRM software](/real-estate-crm-software) for how CRM should sit on inventory.

### Channel partners

- Live portal (not email PDFs)  
- Booking or hold requests with audit trail  
- Fair lead ownership rules  

### Payments on the plot record

- Token and installment tracking  
- GST-aware sales amounts where you need them  
- Export to Tally / accounts—not a promise to replace CA software overnight  

### Multi-project control

- Phase isolation  
- Portfolio roll-up for directors  

## Step 4: Run these five demo tests (non-negotiable)

Bring your own Phase 1 data if possible.

| Test | Pass criteria |
|------|----------------|
| Dual book attempt | Second user sees lock immediately |
| Broker login | Same colors/status as desk |
| Mobile hold | Complete on phone in under two minutes |
| Lead → plot | Cannot save lead without project/plot interest |
| Expire hold | Plot returns to available automatically |

Fail any two tests and walk away—no matter how good the sales deck looks.

## Step 5: Compare total cost, not sticker price

Ask:

- Per-seat vs project vs unlimited users?  
- Cost when you add 20 brokers?  
- Digitization / onboarding fees?  
- Charge for extra projects or phases?  
- Contract length and export rights if you leave?  

INR plans that look cheap can become expensive with seats. Layouts360 starts from ₹2,000/month with unlimited users on tiers—model *your* headcount in the quote.

## Step 6: Check India fit (not US brokerage DNA)

Plot sales in India usually need:

- Field-first mobile use  
- Installment culture  
- Channel partner heavy selling  
- Tier-2/Tier-3 site offices with imperfect connectivity assumptions  
- Language-agnostic UX (clear UI beats English essay screens)

Global CRMs optimized for email nurture often disappoint at the cabin.

## Step 7: Plan rollout before you buy

Software fails when training is an afterthought. Require a written plan:

**Week 1:** Digitize one phase; train site staff on block-and-book.  
**Week 2:** Open partner access; run a mock launch.  
**Month 1:** Leadership reviews sold % from the live map only.

Red flags after go-live: shadow Excel still called “real,” brokers forwarding PDFs, holds that never expire.

## Decision scorecard (copy this)

Score each vendor 1–5:

1. Map quality on *our* layout  
2. Double-booking prevention  
3. Partner portal truthfulness  
4. Mobile cabin usability  
5. CRM + plot linkage  
6. Time to go live  
7. 12-month cost at our headcount  
8. Training / support clarity  

Multiply (1–3) by two—they matter most. Highest weighted score wins.

## FAQ

### What is the best plot management software?

The best tool is the one that passes live lock and partner tests on your layout. Category leaders vary by apartment vs plotted focus—validate with your Phase 1.

### Can small developers afford it?

Yes. Entry INR plans exist specifically so 50–200 plot teams are not forced into enterprise ERP. Start with one phase.

### Do we still need Excel?

Maybe for one-off analysis. Not as the availability master. If Excel remains “real,” the project failed.

### How is this different from inventory software?

Plot management emphasizes the digital layout and plot sales workflow. Inventory management emphasizes portfolio status across unit types. Strong platforms cover both—Layouts360 does.
