If you have heard about **Layouts360** and wondered what it actually is, this guide answers that in plain language—no jargon, no sales fluff.

**Layouts360** is a cloud-based **real estate plot management software** built for layout and plot developers in India. It replaces the usual mix of Excel sheets, WhatsApp updates, and paper maps with one live system your whole team can use on phone or laptop.

## What problem does Layouts360 solve?

Most plot developers run sales like this:

- Inventory lives in an Excel file (often several versions)
- Site plans are shared as PDFs on WhatsApp
- Sales agents call the office to check if a plot is free
- Payments are tracked in another sheet—or in someone's memory

That workflow creates **double bookings**, slow follow-ups, and embarrassed site visits when two customers are shown the same plot.

Layouts360 fixes this by giving everyone the **same live view** of every plot, every booking, and every payment.

## What does Layouts360 do?

### 1. Interactive digital layouts

Upload your layout plan and show it on a **satellite map**. Customers see exactly where a plot sits on real land—not just lines on a flat PDF. Plots are colour-coded: available, booked, sold, or on hold.

### 2. Real-time plot inventory

When an agent blocks or books a plot, the status updates **instantly** for the whole team. No refresh, no phone call to head office, no "sorry, that one was sold an hour ago."

### 3. Real estate CRM built for plots

Track enquiries, follow-ups, and which customer is interested in which plot number. This is **real estate CRM software** that understands phases, plot IDs, and broker leads—not a generic contact list.

### 4. Booking and payment tracking

Record booking amounts, instalments, and outstanding balances in one place. Built for how Indian plot sales actually work—partial payments, token amounts, and scheduled dues.

### 5. Broker and team access

Give sales staff and channel partners controlled access so they can show inventory and log leads without breaking your data.

## Who is Layouts360 for?

Layouts360 is designed for:

- **Layout and plot developers** selling residential or commercial plots
- **Real estate companies** running one or multiple projects
- **Sales teams** that work on-site and need mobile access
- **Builders in India** who want INR pricing and simple onboarding

It is **not** built for apartment rental management, society maintenance billing, or full accounting/ERP replacement. It focuses on **selling plots faster with fewer mistakes**.
