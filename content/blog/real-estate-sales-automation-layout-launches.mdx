**Real estate sales automation** during layout launches is not generic email blasts. It is routing leads, reminding agents, updating pipeline stages, and enforcing inventory rules while hundreds of buyers and brokers act at once.

## What breaks during launches without automation

### Leads stall in inboxes

Portal and walk-in enquiries sit unassigned while the floor team handles chaos.

### Follow-ups slip by hours

In launch season, hours equal lost bookings.

### Inventory promises outpace reality

Agents quote plots that blocked or sold minutes ago.

## Automation that matters for launches

### Lead capture to assignment

Every enquiry ties to project and agent automatically.

### Timed reminders

Calls and site visits scheduled with notifications—see [instant follow-up patterns](/compare/instant-lead-follow-up-real-estate-crm).

### Pipeline tied to plots

Stages reflect real units: inquiry, visit, blocked, booked, sold.

### Inventory-triggered actions

Starting a hold changes layout status for all users.

## Launch-specific automation checklist

1. Pre-register brokers on partner portal
2. Open launch window with block timers published
3. Auto-expire holds that miss token deadline
4. Escalate hot leads to closing team queue
5. Leadership dashboard for sold % live

## Metrics to track

- Lead response time median
- Visit-to-block conversion
- Block-to-booking conversion
- Double-booking incidents (target zero)

## Common mistakes

- Automating emails without inventory context
- Letting partners sell from offline PDFs during launch
- Removing block timers under broker pressure

## Desk vs partner automation

Your internal desk and external brokers need the same triggers with different permissions. Desk staff may convert blocks; partners may only request them. Automation should route approvals to a closing manager queue instead of flooding one WhatsApp admin.

## Integrations you should not delay

- Lead forms on marketing pages feeding CRM directly
- Payment confirmation updating plot status without manual entry
- End-of-day sold report from live data, not retyped totals

## Week-before-launch runbook

Monday: freeze master inventory. Tuesday: partner training on portal. Wednesday: mock launch with ten test blocks. Thursday: publish block policy PDF at site office. Friday: leadership dashboard walkthrough. Launch day: only override with logged reason.
