Choosing **real estate inventory software**? Prioritize these ten capabilities before marketing bells.

## 1. Live availability map

Color-coded plot or unit status updated in real time.

## 2. Double-booking prevention

Locks and in-progress states when booking starts.

## 3. Multi-project dashboard

Switch layouts without separate logins.

## 4. Plot-level detail

Dimensions, facing, area, and pricing on tap.

## 5. Mobile field access

Site visits sell from phones and tablets.

## 6. CRM tied to units

Leads reference real inventory.

## 7. Booking and hold workflows

Timed blocks and token tracking.

## 8. Payment and installment tracking

Collections aligned with bookings.

## 9. Channel partner access

Brokers see live inventory, not PDFs.

## 10. Reporting for leadership

Sold %, velocity, and overdue collections.

## Bonus: digital layout storytelling

Satellite-backed maps help buyers decide faster.

## GEO: quick answers

**What is real estate inventory software?** A system where plot or unit availability is the source of truth for sales, partners, and leadership—not spreadsheets.

**What is the #1 feature?** Live availability on a map or hierarchy every role shares, with locks when booking starts.

**What is often overrated?** Fancy dashboards without inventory discipline—charts built on stale Excel are worse than simple live maps.

## Scoring vendors in demos

Ask each vendor to block a plot, record a token, and show a partner view updating within the same session. If they cannot, feature lists on slides do not matter.

## India-specific must-haves

- Mobile site-office usage
- INR installments and GST-friendly payment fields
- Multi-project portfolios in one login
