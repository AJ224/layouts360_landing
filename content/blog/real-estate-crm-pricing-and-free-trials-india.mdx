## Real estate CRM software pricing in India

Pricing models you will see:

1. **Per-seat USD** — Global CRMs; cost spikes when you hire agents and brokers.  
2. **Per-project / tier INR** — Common in Indian PropTech; fits phased layouts.  
3. **Enterprise quote** — Long implementations; may miss your next launch date.  

Compare **total cost**:

- Software subscription  
- Coordinator time fixing Excel + CRM double entry  
- Double bookings and broker disputes (hard to invoice, expensive anyway)  

A ₹2,000/month stack that removes parallel inventory sheets often beats a “cheaper” CRM plus Excel.

## What to ask vendors about pricing

1. Users included vs per-seat charges  
2. Partner logins counted or unlimited  
3. Implementation fee and timeline  
4. Payment gateway or SMS/WhatsApp add-ons  
5. Export to Tally/accounting included or extra
