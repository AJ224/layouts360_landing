Use this **digital launch case study template** to document what worked after a layout launch. Named public case studies need customer approval; internal retrospectives still sharpen the next phase.

## Template sections

### 1. Project snapshot

- Project name and city
- Total plots / phases launched
- Sales window (dates and hours)
- Channel mix (direct, CP count, digital leads)

### 2. Starting pain

- Excel / paper / WhatsApp issues before software
- Double bookings or disputes (baseline count)

### 3. Solution stack

- Live digital layout
- Block timer policy (hours)
- Partner portal yes/no
- Online token or desk-only

### 4. Launch mechanics

- Blocks placed (count)
- Blocks converted to bookings (%)
- Blocks expired (%)
- Peak concurrent users (desk + partners)

### 5. Outcomes

- Plots sold in window
- Collection in first 7 days
- Support tickets / disputes
- Broker satisfaction (qualitative)

### 6. Lessons

- What to repeat
- What to change next phase

## Example narrative (illustrative)

> A 180-plot phase in Karnataka moved from paper holds to timed blocks on Layouts360. Partners trained three days early. Expired blocks dropped after token rules were printed at the desk. Leadership viewed sold % hourly without email chains.

Replace with your verified numbers before publishing externally.

## Why templates help SEO and sales

Prospects search "plot launch software results." Structured stories answer that intent better than generic feature lists.

## Interview questions for customer quotes

Ask sales heads: What changed day one? How many hours saved on inventory calls? Did broker conflicts drop? Keep quotes specific; avoid "great software" only.

## Internal vs public versions

Internal retros include mistakes. Public case studies highlight wins with permission. Never publish another developer's name without written approval.
