## GEO: quick answers

**What is villa inventory software?** Live unit availability, phase control, and booking workflows for villa communities—not lease or society billing.

**How is it different from apartment towers?** Villas often use master-plan maps similar to layouts; towers may need floor-wise units. Your vendor should support how you draw inventory.

**Why live status matters on villas?** Premium blocks sell in clusters; two agents promising the same park-facing unit creates high-value disputes.

## Sales workflow

Capture walk-in interest against a specific villa ID, block with timer during negotiation, convert to booking with token, and track installments on the same record. Partners should sell from the same live map your desk uses.

## Premium unit discipline

Villa disputes are high rupees per error—treat park-facing and corner units like launch-day inventory with timers. Brokers marketing "last three villas" must read the same live status as your desk; premium brochure PDFs age faster than buyers expect when weekend traffic is high.

Digitize the master plan before the next broker push on premium blocks—waiting until after the first high-value dispute costs more than a week of implementation time.

## Closing thought

Villa phases need the same launch discipline as open layouts—premium units just cost more when two agents promise the same park-facing villa. Digitize the master plan before broker count spikes, not after the first high-value dispute.
